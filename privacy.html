CTG Planting - Privacy Policy

CTG Planting is an internal planting record app used by the employees of Cascade Tropicals / Gold Plants.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, the app receives your name and email address from Google. It uses them only to identify who is logged in and to show your name on planting records. We do not receive your Google password.

How it is used

Your name and email are stored in the company's own Google Sheets together with the planting records you enter. They are not sold, shared with other companies, or used for advertising.
